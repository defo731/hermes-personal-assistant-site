Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Hermes Personal Assistant handles data when the operator connects a Google account.

Google data access and use

Depending on the permissions granted and the request, Hermes can access Calendar event details, Tasks titles and details, and Gmail message information and contents. Hermes uses that information to complete the operator’s requests, such as finding events, managing tasks, and working with email. Actions that change data or send email depend on the permissions granted.

External AI processing

When an external AI model is used to interpret a request or generate a response, the model provider may receive the request and relevant Google data, such as event details, task text, or email excerpts. Such processing supports the requested assistant feature. Google user data must not be used to train or improve a generalized AI model. Provider processing and retention depend on the configured provider and its settings.

Storage and deletion

Google authorization credentials are used by the assistant to maintain the connection. The public site has no sign-in, forms, or analytics scripts and does not receive data from the assistant. You can revoke future access in your Google Account connections. Revoking access does not delete copies already retained. For questions about stored data, retention, or deletion, contact kda731@gmail.com.

Hermes’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
